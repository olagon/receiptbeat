# Play Store listing

**App name:** Receipt Beat

**Short description (80 max):** Scan receipts, find them in any email, and track them until you are paid back.

**Full description:**

Lost receipts? Money you never got back? Receipt Beat keeps every receipt on track until it is reimbursed or claimed on your taxes.

Snap it. Done. Point your camera and Receipt Beat crops and reads the receipt in about 3 seconds. Merchant, date, tax and total, filled in.

Works with any email. Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, Proton and more. Import an export file or connect your account, then keep the receipts it finds with a swipe.

Smart AI, right on your phone. Gemini Nano where your phone has it, and a rules reader on every phone. Your mail stays private.

Tracked until you are paid back. Inbox, Ready, Submitted, Reimbursed. Gentle reminders when something is waiting.

Tax time, sorted. Totals by category with limits applied, and one ZIP for your accountant.

Reports in two taps. PDF, CSV and every photo, ready to send.

Private by design. No ads. No tracking. No account. Open source.

**Category:** Finance

**Data safety form:** No data collected. No data shared. Data is encrypted. Users can request deletion (uninstall or delete in app).
